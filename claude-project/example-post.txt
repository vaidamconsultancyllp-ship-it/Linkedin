Compliance deadlines coming up. Save this post before you forget:

🗓️ 27 Sep – AOC-4 for One Person Companies
OPCs must file FY 2025-26 financial statements. Late fee: ₹100 per day, no upper limit.

🗓️ 30 Sep – Tax Audit Report
For businesses that need a tax audit. No extension notified yet, and a bank strike is planned for 28–30 Sep. Pay dues and file early. Penalty: up to ₹1.5 lakh.

🗓️ 15 Oct – ADT-1 (auditor appointment)
If your AGM is on 30 Sep, tell the ROC about your auditor within 15 days.

🗓️ 29 Oct – AOC-4 | 30 Oct – LLP Form 8
Annual financial statements for companies, and Statement of Accounts & Solvency for LLPs. Both cost ₹100 per day if late.

✅ Good news: DIR-3 KYC is now once every 3 years
Directors who completed KYC for FY 2025-26 are next due by 30 June 2028.

Dates as per current notifications; check for any extension.
